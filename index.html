A few important things

Terms of Service

Please read these guidelines before submitting a request.

Revisions

1–2 minor revisions are available during the sketch stage. Major changes or changes requested after approval may require an additional fee, if agreed in advance.

Refund policy

Full refunds are available only if work has not started. No refunds once the sketch is approved. If the artist cannot finish for personal reasons, a partial refund will be discussed.

Usage rights

Commissioned artwork is for personal use only unless commercial rights are purchased. You may post the artwork with proper credit. Do not resell, claim authorship, or use the artwork commercially without permission. Do not use it for AI training.

Payment & workflow

Payment method, deposit amount, and payment schedule will be confirmed before work begins. A request is not accepted until the artist confirms it.

Turnaround & deadlines

Turnaround time depends on complexity and current workload. A deadline is not confirmed until both parties agree to it.

File delivery

Final files are delivered digitally. PNG or JPEG delivery can be agreed upon; editable source files may require an additional fee.

Artist rights

The artist may decline a commission before work begins and may post commissioned artwork on social media, unless otherwise agreed in writing.
